Help & Guide (رہنمائی)

1. How Offline AI Works (آف لائن ماڈل کیسے چلتا ہے؟)

This application downloads a quantized, highly-efficient language model directly into your mobile browser's local CacheStorage and IndexedDB. Once installed, it processes everything locally on your phone without sending any text to external servers!

2. Admin Brain Training (دماغی تربیت)

Go to the Admin Brain tab to teach the AI custom facts, rules, or answers. Whatever you enter is stored permanently in the phone's memory. When asked in Roman Urdu, the AI recognizes the taught knowledge and provides the exact Urdu answer!

3. Live Internet Fallback (لائیو انٹرنیٹ تلاش)

If a question is not available in the offline model and you are connected to the internet, you can toggle the globe icon (🌐) or let the app search Google in real-time, returning pure Urdu answers!

4. Low-End Phone Optimization (OPPO A15s / 4GB RAM)

If your phone has 4GB RAM, we recommend the SmolLM2 135M model or the Built-in Instant Engine. These run with zero lag, instant startup, and zero thermal throttling.
